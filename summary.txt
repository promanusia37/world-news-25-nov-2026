WORLD NEWS — WEDNESDAY, 25 NOVEMBER 2026
Summary of the stories drawn in entry.png (one image, 1024x1024).

LEAD — STRAIT OF HORMUZ
Two oil tankers struck naval mines, exploded and caught fire while
transiting the Strait of Hormuz, Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard
Corps said. The vessels had been attempting to transit an unauthorised
route through the strait, according to the IRGC statement.
Sources: Just Security, "Early Edition: November 25, 2026"; AP.

WAR — SAUDI ARABIA
Yemen's Iran-aligned Houthis said they attacked Saudi Arabia's Ras
Tanura oil refinery; spokesman Yahya Saree said the attack caused
fires. A day earlier, a Houthi strike on a skyscraper in Riyadh
injured 11 people, Saudi Civil Defense said.
Sources: Just Security Early Edition, 25 Nov 2026; Reuters.

TECH — UNITED STATES
A US federal court found China's Huawei guilty of conspiracy to steal
trade secrets and bank fraud, but not guilty of the overarching
racketeering charge.
Sources: Just Security Early Edition, 25 Nov 2026; Reuters.

CLIMATE — VATICAN
Pope Leo XIV expressed concern over the failure of the UN COP31
climate talks in Antalya to reach an agreement, saying "extreme
environmental events linked to the climate crisis are becoming
increasingly frequent."
Source: AP News, via Just Security Early Edition, 25 Nov 2026.

ECONOMY — UNITED STATES
The US economy roared ahead in the third quarter, while inflation
remained high in October. The Trump administration proposed surprise
new tariffs on Brazil imports, including a 10% duty on aircraft.
Sources: Reuters; LA Post, 25 Nov 2026.

SPACE — CHINA
China's Shenzhou-24 docked at its space station carrying a Pakistani
national as the first foreign astronaut aboard a Chinese mission.
Source: Reuters, 25 Nov 2026.
